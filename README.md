# IronPath

**A phone-friendly app that builds a day-by-day triathlon training plan and adjusts it when life gets in the way.**

You tell IronPath which race you're training for (Olympic distance, half Ironman, full Ironman, or a custom distance), when it is, how experienced you are, how many days a week you can train, and your best recent times. It then builds a full plan of swim, bike, run, combined bike-then-run, and strength sessions leading up to race day. It can be added to a phone's home screen like a regular app.

## What you can do with it

- Create an account and answer a few questions to get a complete plan up to race day.
- See today's workout, and a calendar of the weeks ahead.
- Open any workout for its warm-up, main set and cool-down, with target speeds or effort levels worked out from your own times.
- Mark sessions done, skip them, move them to another day, or swap the sport.
- Ease off a week, or rebuild the plan from today if you fall behind.
- Track your progress: weekly training time, how many sessions you completed, and time spent on each sport.

There is also an optional AI coach (a chat assistant) you can ask questions about your plan. The plan itself is built by fixed rules in the app, not by AI.
